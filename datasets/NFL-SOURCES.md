Founding dates use the explicitly documented basis in each entity. A franchise award/admission is not necessarily the original organization date. Cardinals origins are 1898; Bears predecessor origins are 1919; Packers organized on 1919-08-11; Lions predecessor origins are 1928; Rams predecessor origins are 1936; the 49ers commemorate founding in 1946 although the Hall of Fame lists an AAFC franchise grant in 1944. No month/day is invented for year-only origins.

The Rams have a one-day source disagreement: [Hall of Fame](https://www.profootballhof.com/teams/los-angeles-rams/team-facts) gives 1937-02-12; [Rams official history](https://www.therams.com/news/the-rams-story) gives 1937-02-13. Both dates and sources are preserved, and the observation is marked disputed.
